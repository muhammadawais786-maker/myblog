CryptoBrief is an independent website that publishes daily crypto news, live market data and plain-English guides. Our goal is simple: tell you what moved the market and why, in a few minutes, without the jargon.

## What we publish

- **News** on Bitcoin, Ethereum, altcoins, DeFi and regulation.
- **Live markets** with prices, charts, a heatmap and a coin screener.
- **Guides** that explain the terms and ideas behind the headlines.

## How we work

- We write our own stories and explain things in our own words.
- We link to or name the source for facts and figures, and quote prices at the time of writing.
- If we get something wrong, we correct it and say so. To report a mistake, use the [contact page](/contact/).
- We do not sell positive coverage. If a post is sponsored or contains affiliate links, it will say so clearly.

## How we make money

CryptoBrief is free to read. The site is supported by advertising. Ads are labelled, and they do not change what we write.

## Not financial advice

Nothing on this site is a recommendation to buy or sell any asset. Read our [disclaimer](/disclaimer/).
