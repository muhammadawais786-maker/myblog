CryptoBrief is a daily crypto news site. Every day we publish short, clear stories about what moved the market and why, with the live prices and charts right next to the story so you can check the numbers yourself.

## What you will find here

- **News:** the main stories in Bitcoin, Ethereum, altcoins, DeFi and regulation.
- **Markets:** a live price chart, a heatmap of the whole market and a coin screener.
- **Learn:** short guides that explain the terms you keep seeing, from staking to market cap.

## A note on prices

Prices in the charts come from TradingView and can be a few seconds or minutes behind the exchanges. They are fine for following the market, but not for placing trades. When a story quotes a price, it is the price at the time of writing.
