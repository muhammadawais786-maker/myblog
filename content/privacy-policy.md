This policy explains what happens to your information when you visit CryptoBrief. We keep data collection to a minimum.

## Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name or email address when you browse. When you visit, our hosting provider and the services described below may automatically receive technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, the pages you view and the time of your visit.

If you email us, we receive your email address and the contents of your message, and we use them only to reply.

## Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files stored in your browser. Our site and the third-party services on it may use cookies or similar technologies to work properly, measure usage and show advertising. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some parts of the site may not work as expected if you do.

## Third-party services

- **Hosting and delivery.** The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes requests and may keep server logs for security and performance.
- **Market data.** Live prices and charts are provided by TradingView widgets. Loading a widget connects your browser to TradingView, which may collect data under its own privacy policy.
- **Advertising.** We show ads from advertising networks such as Adsterra. These networks may use cookies and device identifiers to show ads, limit how often you see them and measure results. Their use of your data is covered by their own privacy policies.
- **Fonts.** We load fonts from Google Fonts.

We do not control these third parties. Please read their policies to see how they handle your data.

## How we use information

We use the information above to run and secure the site, understand which content is useful, and pay for the site through advertising. We do not sell your personal information.

## Your choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what data we hold about you, or to ask us to correct or delete it. Contact us at {{< email >}} and we will respond. You can also limit ad tracking with your browser settings or your device's privacy controls.

## Children

CryptoBrief is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the bottom of the page shows when it was last changed.
